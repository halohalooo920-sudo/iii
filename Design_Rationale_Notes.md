## 1. 为什么不走纯粹的小组 Life Design，而是「先探索、后整合」

**核心论点**
Life Design / CCT 的叙事整合，默认来访者**已经有足够可叙事的素材**。但本项目对象是 20–26 岁、正处探索期、对自身兴趣与价值观尚不清晰的 emerging adults。若一开始就要求他们叙事、构建 life theme，产出多半是空的。因此先用结构化的自我探索（RIASEC 兴趣、优势、价值观）把**原始素材**铺出来，再在 S6 整合成 life portrait——先给他们「拿什么来叙事」。

**理论落点（CCT 自身架构就支持这个顺序）**
- CCT 把自我分三层：actor（特质/类型）→ agent（适应/行动）→ author（叙事/生命主题）。
- RIASEC、VIA、价值观采集的是 **actor 层**素材；life portrait 整合是 **author 层**工作。
- 「先 actor 后 author」正是沿着 CCT 自己的层次推进，不是偏离 Life Design，而是为叙事做准备。

**还回应了两个现实约束**
- 6 人 Zoom 小组，难以给每人足够的一对一叙事时间，也不好兜住完整 CCI（尤其 early recollections）激起的强情绪。
- 因此分工：**小组**做广度探索 + 整合；**S6 后的个别辅导（每人 1 小时）**承接一对一的 Life Design 叙事深挖。这才是项目里最纯粹的 Life Design 环节。

**English version (report-ready)**
> Life Design counselling presumes that clients already possess narratable material from which a life theme can be co-constructed. The present participants, however, are emerging adults (aged roughly 20–26) in an exploratory phase who are often still unclear about their own interests, strengths, and values. Asking them to narrate a coherent life theme at the outset would likely yield thin material. The programme therefore front-loads structured self-exploration (interests via RIASEC, strengths, and values) to generate the raw material, and only then integrates it into a life portrait in Session 6 — giving members something to narrate with. This sequence follows Career Construction Theory's own actor–agent–author architecture, gathering "actor" material before "author" integration, rather than departing from it. It also reflects two practical constraints of a six-member online group: the limited time available for one-to-one narrative work, and the difficulty of safely holding the deeper affect that the full Career Construction Interview can evoke. Accordingly, the group carries the breadth of exploration and integration, while the individual counselling sessions (one hour per member, after Session 6) provide the space for deeper, one-to-one Life Design narrative work.
