# Session Design — For Supervisor Review

**Programme:** Self-Exploration and Career Direction for Young Adults Navigating Uncertainty

**Student:** Luo Yujia | **Supervisor:** Mr. Keith Pang | **Date:** May 2026

---

## Programme Overview

- **Sessions:** 9 × 2 hours = 18 hours total
- **Format:** Online via Zoom + Miro collaborative canvas
- **Frequency:** 2–3 times per week
- **Timeline:** Late May to late June 2026
- **Individual counselling:** 1 hour per member, scheduled after Session 6
- **Theoretical framework:** Career Construction Theory (Savickas, 2019)
- **Methodology:** Group-adapted Life Design Counseling — using experiential activities to collect material (interests, strengths, values, personality), identify life themes, and derive direction

---

## Design Logic

The programme follows CCT's three tasks: **organize → impose direction → make meaning**.

The exploration ("organize") phase is deliberately the richest part — five sessions (S2-S6), each exploring a distinct career-exploration element with its own experiential activity. This is where the real work of building self-knowledge happens. Once self-knowledge is gathered and integrated, the "impose direction → action" phase is kept tight (S7-S8): narrow to a direction, then commit to a concrete step. The closing session (S9) completes "make meaning."

## Session Structure (2 hours each, no fixed break)

| Time | Section | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Check-in | Brief sharing linked to previous homework |
| 0:08–0:15 | Review + Introduction | Link to today's theme (facilitator speaks ≤ 5 min) |
| 0:15–0:55 | Anchor Activity | Core experiential exercise (member-led) |
| 0:55–1:40 | Discussion / Sharing | Connect activity to personal life (flexible) |
| 1:40–1:55 | Miro Recording | Document discoveries on canvas |
| 1:55–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

> Note: Reference framework only; proportions adjust per session. No fixed break; members may pause anytime.

## Session 1 — Opening & Connection

**Objectives:** Build group safety; establish norms; activate Career Concern

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Self-introduction + Icebreaker | Each person 2 min: name, current status, why here |
| 0:15–0:35 | Group norms co-creation | Sticky notes on Miro: "What do I need to feel safe to share?" Integrate into 5-7 norms |
| 0:35–0:45 | Confidentiality & recording explanation | Limits of confidentiality; recording policy; confirm consent |
| 0:45–1:10 | "My current situation" timeline + Needs | Draw timeline on Miro (past 1 year → now → next 1 year; mark: attempts made, where stuck, what hoped for). Then 2 sticky notes: "What I hope to take away" + "What I'm afraid this group becomes" |
| 1:10–1:50 | Sharing | Each person 3-4 min: share timeline + needs. Facilitator reflects briefly |
| 1:50–2:00 | Closing | Facilitator reflects whole-group patterns |

**Homework:** This week, notice one thing others find fun or exciting but you feel nothing about. Note what it was and what you felt.

---

## Session 2 — Interests: What Gives Me Energy?

**Objectives:** Expand interest vocabulary via RIASEC; identify patterns from flow experiences

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Check-in | Share homework: "that thing others find fun but you felt nothing about" |
| 0:10–0:30 | RIASEC discussion | One-sentence intro: "We explore not 'what job' but 'what activities give me energy.'" 6 zones on Miro; members place name on 1-2 zones; share why |
| 0:30–0:50 | Flow moments recall | Silently write 3 flow experiences (what/who/where/body sensation); facilitator models one first |
| 0:50–1:15 | Dyad sharing (breakout) | Pairs share 1 flow moment; listener asks open questions only; 6-7 min each |
| 1:15–1:45 | Pattern identification + sharing | Write interest patterns in verbs on Miro (e.g., "solving problems," "creating things"). Willing members share |
| 1:45–2:00 | Closing | Facilitator reflects; one-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** Ask a friend or family member: "What do you think I look most natural/effortless doing?" Note their answer.

---

## Session 3 — Strengths & Skills: What Am I Good At?

**Objectives:** Distinguish strengths from skills; use peer feedback to surface blind spots

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Check-in | Share homework: "What did your friend say you look natural doing?" |
| 0:10–0:25 | Personal brainstorm | One-sentence intro: "Strengths = what feels easy to you but hard for others." Miro: (1) What do people ask me for help with? (2) What feels easy to me? (3) What trained skills do I have? |
| 0:25–0:40 | VIA vocabulary assist | Pick 5 from the 24 character-strengths list that feel most like you |
| 0:40–1:15 | Strengths Spotting | Each member spotlighted 3 min; others give 1 specific observation from S1-2 interactions |
| 1:15–1:35 | Personal integration | Combine self-view + peer-view + trained skills on Miro |
| 1:35–1:50 | "I have nothing" processing | Normalise self-doubt; invite it to be voiced without judgment |
| 1:50–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** Deliberately use one strength this week; note how it felt.

---

## Session 4 — Values: What Truly Matters to Me?

**Objectives:** Surface core values through forced choice; distinguish personal vs imposed values

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Check-in | Share homework |
| 0:08–0:15 | Introduction | Values vs goals vs interests (with example) |
| 0:15–0:35 | Values Card Sort | 50 cards → 3 tiers on Miro; must distribute; follow gut reaction; mark top 5-7 |
| 0:35–0:55 | Source-auditing | For each top value: "Is this mine, or did someone put it there?" Mark source |
| 0:55–1:10 | Values conflict | Identify 1 pair of conflicting values; write how you usually handle it |
| 1:10–1:50 | Dyad sharing + group discussion | Pairs (breakout, 15 min): share top values, source labels, conflict pair. Then main room (25 min): share discoveries; facilitator connects across members ("Did anyone else find this?") |
| 1:50–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** This week, observe one choice you make — which value drove it?

---

## Session 5 — Role Models & Qualities: Who Do I Want to Become?

**Objectives:** Use admired figures to surface the personal qualities/identity the member wants to embody (life-theme material)

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Check-in | Share homework: the choice you observed and the value behind it |
| 0:10–0:18 | Introduction | "The people we admire often reflect who we want to become. Today we look at your role models — and what they say about you." |
| 0:18–0:45 | Role model adjectives | On Miro, each person names 2-3 people they admire (real, fictional, public, or known personally). For each, write 3-5 adjectives describing what attracts you to them. Then circle adjectives that repeat across figures or resonate most |
| 0:45–1:20 | Triad discussion (breakout) | Each shares their adjectives. Listeners gently probe beneath surface words — e.g., if someone says "pretty," ask "what does that mean to you — confidence? being well-liked?" Goal: get to the real quality underneath |
| 1:20–1:40 | Personal integration | On Miro, write "Qualities I want to embody": which of these do I already have? Which do I wish I had more of? |
| 1:40–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** This week, notice one moment when you felt most like the person you want to be. What were you doing?

---

## Session 6 — Integration: What Do I See?

**Objectives:** Synthesise S2-5 discoveries through visual/intuitive methods; identify possible directions

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Check-in | Share homework: that moment you felt most like yourself |
| 0:08–0:13 | Introduction + drawing demo | Show facilitator's rough example; rules (stick figures OK, no right/wrong, silence) |
| 0:13–0:20 | "A Day in My Life" drawing | Guided: "5 years from now, no limits, a normal Wednesday…" Draw 7 min in silence |
| 0:20–0:45 | Sharing + Debrief | Each shows drawing, shares most striking image; facilitator reflects; "What value is hidden here?" |
| 0:45–1:00 | CCI questions (2) | (1) Favourite story/film — what's it about? (2) A motto you live by? Write keywords on Miro (role-model adjectives from S5 already on canvas as further life-theme material) |
| 1:00–1:30 | Triad discussion (breakout) | Bring together drawing + adjectives + story/motto. Listeners ask: "What direction do you see emerging? What attracts you? Which value?" |
| 1:30–1:45 | Narrowing | On Miro, write 2-3 possible selves / directions that feel exciting or scary |
| 1:45–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence: "A direction I can now see is…" |

**Homework:** Contact one person already working in a direction of interest; chat 15 min.

**Individual counselling begins after this session.**

---

## Session 7 — Direction Focus

**Objectives:** Test the imagined direction against reality; narrow to one direction to pursue

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Check-in (extended) | Share reality-check conversation: who did you talk to? What surprised you? Was it like you imagined? |
| 0:15–0:40 | "Imagination vs reality" | On Miro, two columns: left = how I imagined this direction; right = what I actually learned. Where do they match? Where's the gap? Does the gap pull you closer or make you hesitate? |
| 0:40–1:15 | Triad discussion (breakout) | Each shares their comparison. Listeners listen + ask open questions ("What does that gap feel like?" "Are you more curious or more hesitant now?") |
| 1:15–1:40 | Narrow to a direction | On Miro, each writes one sentence: "The direction I most want to move toward is___." Does not need to be 100% certain |
| 1:40–2:00 | Closing | Each one sentence: "Right now I'm leaning toward…" |

**Homework:** Live with this direction for one week. Notice: any moments that confirm it? Any that make you doubt it?

---

## Session 8 — My Next Step

**Objectives:** Confirm direction; commit to one concrete, values-aligned action step

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Check-in (extended) | "Living with your direction this week — what did you notice? Confirming moments? Doubting moments?" |
| 0:15–0:30 | Confirm/update direction | Each writes final direction statement on Miro. Changes from last week are OK |
| 0:30–0:55 | Write Action Plan | One concrete step in next 2 weeks. 5 fields: What / Why (which value) / When / Done (how I'll know) / Block (what might stop me + plan) |
| 0:55–1:05 | Self-check | "Can I start this tomorrow?" If no → break smaller |
| 1:05–1:45 | Public sharing + response | Each shares their plan (3 min). Others respond freely — a word of support, a connection noticed, or "I heard you" |
| 1:45–1:55 | Group reflection | "Confidence 1-10? What would make it +1?" |
| 1:55–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** Complete your action step within 2 weeks. Also: look back through your full Miro map (S1-S8) before the last session.

---

## Session 9 — Integration & Farewell

**Objectives:** Complete make meaning; integrate the journey into a personal narrative

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Check-in (extended) | "Did you do your action step? What happened?" No judgment, just listen |
| 0:12–0:35 | Review full Miro Map | Each views their S1-S8 journey for 20 min. Prompt: "How did you get from S1 to here? What changed? What didn't?" |
| 0:35–0:55 | Letter to future self | 5 prompts: (1) One thing I now know about myself… (2) I'm still struggling with… (3) I promise in 1 year I will… (4) Thank you to this group for… (5) If future me feels lost again, remember… |
| 0:55–1:25 | Group sharing (voluntary) | Read 1-2 segments. Others listen + reflect |
| 1:25–1:45 | Written feedback to each other | Miro shared area: each writes one sentence to every other member (specific observation + wish) |
| 1:45–1:55 | Closing ceremony | Each raises a cup/candle. Facilitator: "Together we completed…" Members: "I take away…" Three deep breaths |
| 1:55–2:00 | Post-test + goodbye | Send post-test link; mention optional 3-month follow-up |

---

## Miro Canvas Accumulation

Each member builds their Career Construction Map across sessions:
- S1: Current situation + attempts made
- S2: Interests / what gives me energy
- S3: Strengths & skills
- S4: Values (with source labels)
- S5: Role-model adjectives → qualities I want to embody
- S6: Drawing + CCI keywords + possible directions
- S7: Imagination-vs-reality + chosen direction
- S8: Action plan
- S9: Full integration + letter to future self

---

## Evaluation Strategies

| Tool | Timing |
|---|---|
| Self-clarity scaling questions (5 items, 1-10) | Pre-test (before S1) + Post-test (after S9) |
| Miro canvas progression tracking | Every session |
| Facilitator observation notes | After every session |
| Member reflection & action plan | Sessions 8-9 |
| Individual counselling progress | After Session 6 |
