# Session Design — For Supervisor Review

**Programme:** Self-Exploration and Career Direction for Young Adults Navigating Uncertainty

**Student:** Luo Yujia | **Supervisor:** Mr. Keith Pang | **Date:** May 2026

---

## Programme Overview

- **Sessions:** 9 × 2 hours = 18 hours total
- **Format:** Online via Zoom + Miro collaborative canvas
- **Frequency:** 2–3 times per week
- **Timeline:** Late May to late June 2026
- **Individual counselling:** 1 hour per member, scheduled after Session 5
- **Theoretical framework:** Career Construction Theory (Savickas, 2019)
- **Methodology:** Group-adapted Life Design Counseling — using experiential activities to collect material (interests, strengths, values), identify life themes, and derive direction

---

## Session Structure (2 hours each, no fixed break)

| Time | Section | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Check-in | Brief sharing linked to previous homework |
| 0:08–0:15 | Review + Introduction | Link to today's theme (facilitator speaks ≤ 5 min) |
| 0:15–0:55 | Anchor Activity | Core experiential exercise (member-led) |
| 0:55–1:40 | Discussion / Sharing | Connect activity to personal life (flexible) |
| 1:40–1:55 | Miro Recording | Document discoveries on canvas |
| 1:55–2:00 | Homework + Closing | One-sentence check-out |

## Session 1 — Opening & Connection

**Objectives:** Build group safety; establish norms; activate Career Concern

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Self-introduction + Icebreaker | Each person 2 min: name, current status, why here |
| 0:15–0:35 | Group norms co-creation | Sticky notes on Miro: "What do I need to feel safe to share?" |
| 0:35–0:45 | Confidentiality & recording explanation | Limits of confidentiality; recording policy; confirm consent |
| 0:45–1:10 | "My current situation" timeline + Needs | Draw timeline on Miro (past 1 year → now → next 1 year; mark: attempts, where stuck, what hoped for). Then add 2 sticky notes beside it: "What I hope to take away from this group" + "What I'm afraid this group becomes" |
| 1:10–1:50 | Sharing | Each person 3-4 min: share timeline + needs. Others listen. Facilitator reflects briefly |
| 1:50–2:00 | Closing | Facilitator reflects whole-group patterns; homework assigned |

**Homework:** This week, notice one thing that others find fun or exciting but you feel nothing about. Note: what it was, and what you felt (or didn't feel).

---

## Session 2 — Interests: What Gives Me Energy?

**Objectives:** Expand interest vocabulary via RIASEC; identify patterns from flow experiences

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Check-in | Share homework: "that thing others find fun but you felt nothing about" — brief discussion of what this tells you |
| 0:10–0:30 | RIASEC discussion | One-sentence intro: "Today we explore not 'what job' but 'what activities give me energy.'" 6 zones on Miro with descriptions; members place name on 1-2 zones; each shares why (1 min) |
| 0:30–0:50 | Flow moments recall | Silently write 3 flow experiences (what/who/where/body sensation); facilitator models one first (1 min) |
| 0:50–1:15 | Dyad sharing (breakout rooms) | Pairs share 1 flow moment; listener asks open questions only; 6-7 min each |
| 1:15–1:45 | Pattern identification + sharing | Back in main room. Each writes interest patterns in verbs on Miro (e.g., "solving problems," "creating visual things"). Willing members share aloud |
| 1:45–2:00 | Closing | Facilitator reflects; one-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** Ask a friend or family member: "What do you think I look most natural/effortless doing?" Note their answer.

**Miro output:** S2 section: 3 flow moments + interest patterns (in verb form)

---

## Session 3 — Strengths: What Am I Good At?

**Objectives:** Distinguish strengths from skills; use peer feedback to surface blind spots

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Check-in | Share homework: "What did your friend/family say you look natural doing?" |
| 0:10–0:25 | Personal brainstorm | One-sentence intro: "Strengths ≠ what you do a lot; it's what feels easy to you but hard for others." Miro: (1) What do people ask me for help with? (2) What feels easy to me? (3) What trained skills do I have? |
| 0:25–0:40 | VIA vocabulary assist | Pick 5 from 24 character strengths list that feel most like you |
| 0:40–1:15 | Strengths Spotting | Each member spotlighted 3 min; others give 1 specific observation from S1-2 interactions |
| 1:15–1:35 | Personal integration | Combine self-view + peer-view + trained skills on Miro |
| 1:35–1:50 | "I have nothing" processing | Normalise self-doubt; invite it to be voiced without judgment |
| 1:50–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** Deliberately use one strength this week; note how it felt.

**Miro output:** S3 section: self-brainstorm + VIA picks + peer observations + skills

---

## Session 4 — Values: What Truly Matters to Me?

**Objectives:** Surface core values through forced choice; distinguish personal vs imposed values

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Check-in | Share homework |
| 0:08–0:15 | Introduction | Values vs goals vs interests (with example) |
| 0:15–0:35 | Values Card Sort | 50 cards → 3 tiers on Miro; must distribute; follow gut reaction; mark top 5-7 |
| 0:35–0:55 | Source-auditing | For each top value: "Is this mine, or did someone put it there?" Mark source |
| 0:55–1:10 | Values conflict | Identify 1 pair of conflicting values; write how you usually handle it |
| 1:10–1:50 | Dyad sharing + group discussion | Pairs (breakout, 15 min): share top values, source labels, conflict pair. Partner asks open questions. Then back in main room (25 min): share discoveries; facilitator connects across members ("Did anyone else find this?") |
| 1:50–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** This week, observe one choice you make — which value drove it?

---

## Session 5 — Integration: What Do I See?

**Objectives:** Synthesise S2-4 discoveries through visual/intuitive methods; identify possible directions

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Check-in | Share homework |
| 0:08–0:13 | Introduction + drawing demo | Show facilitator's rough example; explain rules (stick figures OK, no right/wrong, silence) |
| 0:13–0:20 | "A Day in My Life" drawing | Guided: "5 years from now, no limits, a normal Wednesday…" Draw 7 min in silence |
| 0:20–0:40 | Sharing + Debrief | Each person shows drawing, shares most striking image; facilitator reflects; ask: "What value is hidden in this picture?" |
| 0:40–0:55 | Career Construction Interview (3 questions) | (1) Admired figure — what trait? (2) Favourite story — what's it about? (3) Motto? Write keywords on Miro |
| 0:55–1:25 | Triad discussion (breakout) | Each shares 2-3 possible directions from drawing + CCI; listeners ask: what attracts you? which value? body reaction? |
| 1:25–1:45 | Narrowing down | Upload drawing to Miro; write 2-3 possible selves that feel exciting or scary |
| 1:45–1:55 | Whole-group sharing | One sentence each: "The direction I can now see is…" |
| 1:55–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** Contact one person already working in your direction of interest; chat 15 min.

**Individual counselling begins after this session.**

---

## Session 6 — Exploring and Narrowing

**Objectives:** Deepen exploration of one possible self; compare against values and strengths; identify what's still uncertain

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Check-in | Share reality-check conversation: what surprised you most? |
| 0:10–0:20 | Review S5 possible selves | Each person looks at their 2-3 possible selves on Miro. Any changes after reality check? Anything new? |
| 0:20–0:50 | "Possible Self Deep Dive" | Each chooses 1 possible self to explore deeply. Fill structured template on Miro: (1) What would I be doing day-to-day? (2) Which of my values does this satisfy? (3) Which of my strengths does this use? (4) What am I still uncertain about? (5) What do I need to get one step closer? |
| 0:50–1:25 | Triad discussion (breakout) | Each person 10 min: share deep dive. Listeners ask: "Are you genuinely drawn to this, or do you feel you should be?" "Does this connect to your S5 drawing?" |
| 1:25–1:45 | Whole-group reflection | "After this deep dive, is your direction clearer or more uncertain?" Both are OK. Facilitator normalises uncertainty |
| 1:45–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** "If you were to take one small step toward this direction, what would it be? Think about it and bring it to next session."

---

## Session 7 — Refining Direction and Preparing for Action

**Objectives:** Refine direction based on exploration; identify the smallest actionable next step

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Check-in | "What small step did you think of? Share with the group" |
| 0:10–0:30 | Direction refinement | Each person writes on Miro: "After two weeks of exploring, the direction I most want to pursue is___." One clear sentence |
| 0:30–0:50 | "What needs to happen" brainstorm | "If you want to take one step toward this direction, what needs to happen?" List everything (big and small, realistic and not). Write all on Miro sticky notes |
| 0:50–1:20 | Dyad discussion (breakout) | Pairs help each other: from the brainstorm list, pick "the smallest thing you could do in 2 weeks." Partner asks: "Can you start this right now? If not, what's even smaller?" |
| 1:20–1:45 | Group sharing | Each person: "My next step is___." Others respond: "I heard you. This connects to your [value]." |
| 1:45–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** Prepare your action step in detail (What / Why / When / How you'll know it's done / What might block you). Bring to S8 for public commitment.

---

## Session 8 — My Next Step

**Objectives:** Concretise direction into a 14-day actionable step; build confidence through public commitment

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Check-in | Share NVC practice |
| 0:08–0:15 | Introduction | "A step" = not "find a job" but "Wednesday, message X for coffee." Must be startable + completable in 14 days |
| 0:15–0:45 | Write action plan | 5 fields: What / Why (which value) / When / Done (how I know it's complete) / Block (what might stop me + plan) |
| 0:45–0:55 | Self-check | "Can I start this right now?" If no → break smaller |
| 0:55–1:30 | Public commitment | Each 5 min: announce action. Others respond: (1) I heard you (2) This connects to your [value] (3) How can I support you? |
| 1:30–1:45 | Accountability buddy pairing | Pairs commit to check in once within 2 weeks |
| 1:45–1:55 | Group reflection | "Confidence 1-10? What would make it +1?" |
| 1:55–2:00 | Closing | One-sentence check-out |

**Homework:** Complete committed action within 2 weeks; check in with buddy.

---

## Session 9 — Integration & Farewell

**Objectives:** Complete make meaning; integrate the journey into a personal narrative

| Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Check-in (extended) | "Did you do your committed action?" No judgment, just listen |
| 0:12–0:35 | Review full Miro Map | Each person views their S1-S8 journey for 20 min. Prompt: "How did you get from S1 to here? What changed? What didn't?" |
| 0:35–0:55 | Letter to future self | 5 prompts: (1) One thing I now know about myself… (2) I'm still struggling with… (3) I promise in 1 year I will… (4) Thank you to this group for… (5) If future me feels lost again, remember… |
| 0:55–1:25 | Group sharing (voluntary) | Read 1-2 segments. Others listen + reflect |
| 1:25–1:45 | Written feedback to each other | Miro shared area: each person writes one sentence to every other member (specific observation + wish) |
| 1:45–1:55 | Closing ceremony | Each raises a cup/candle. Facilitator: "Together we completed…" Members: "I take away…" Three deep breaths |
| 1:55–2:00 | Post-test + goodbye | Send post-test link; mention optional 3-month follow-up |

---

## Miro Canvas Accumulation

Each member builds their Career Construction Map across sessions:
- S1: Current situation + attempts made
- S2: Interests / what gives me energy
- S3: Strengths / skills
- S4: Values (with source labels)
- S5: Drawing + possible directions
- S6: Possible Self Deep Dive (values/strengths alignment + uncertainties)
- S7: Refined direction statement + "what needs to happen"
- S8: Action plan
- S9: Full integration + letter

---

## Evaluation Strategies

| Tool | Timing |
|---|---|
| Self-clarity scaling questions (5 items, 1-10) | Pre-test (before S1) + Post-test (after S9) |
| Miro canvas progression tracking | Every session |
| Facilitator observation notes | After every session |
| Member reflection & action plan presentation | Session 9 |
| Individual counselling progress | After Session 5 |
